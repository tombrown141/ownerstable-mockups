The
Owners
Table

A community of small business owners, hosted by Chase, where members get to know each other, get practical help moving their businesses forward, and share ideas to make their communities stronger. The Owners Table is organized in local chapters, starting in Austin.

What happens at the table

FPOFinal: Two owners comparing notes over coffee

Learn from each other

Owners share what’s working and what’s been challenging lately: pricing, cash flow, finding staff for the holiday push. Someone at the table has usually been through it.

FPOFinal: An expert taking questions, owners leaning in

The experts you need

Chase brings in people who can help with the questions owners face most, like finding staff who care as much as you do, keeping supplies moving when everything is shifting, and working through local licenses and permits.

FPOFinal: A handshake across the table, business cards out

Open doors for each other

Members make introductions to customers, suppliers, lenders, and local organizations. Chase does too.

FPOFinal: An owner speaking, the room listening

Make our communities stronger

Good things happen when owners connect. Ideas from the table help Chase find better ways to serve small businesses, and can spark partnerships with local nonprofits, organizations, and leaders who share owners’ goals.

How we gather

A few things hold true at every chapter’s table.

  • 01

    Built around what owners need

    Chase plans each gathering around what members say matters most, and leaves plenty of room for owners to talk with each other.

  • 02

    Gatherings close to home

    Chase hosts gatherings around the city, including at members’ businesses.

  • 03

    Every owner gets a say

    Gatherings are built for conversation, so every owner at the table has a chance to speak.

  • 04

    We stay connected between gatherings

    Members keep the conversation going online, with questions, answers, and introductions. Coming in 2027.

  • 05

    Bring a guest

    Members can bring another owner to a gathering.Optional, if the team adopts this ritual.

Questions owners ask

How do I join?

Tell us you’re interested through the Contact us form. We’ll review every message and follow up.

Who runs the Owners Table?

Chase started the Owners Table and runs it: it organizes the gatherings, brings in experts, and manages the community. Members help shape it, by telling us what matters to them, what to take up next, and how the community should grow.

What does Chase ask of members?

Honest feedback, and your perspective when issues affect your business or your community. You decide how much you share.

Will my name or story be shared?

Not without your permission. Each member decides how their own story is told.

What happens at a gathering?

Owners talk through what’s on their minds, hear from experts, and get to know each other. Gatherings are about supporting local small businesses and building a community together.

Do I need to bank with Chase?

No.

Is there a cost?

No.

Where is the Owners Table?

The Owners Table is organized in local chapters. Austin is the founding chapter, and San Francisco follows in spring 2027. Each chapter has its own members and gatherings, and Chase hosts every one.

Will there be a chapter in my city?

We’re starting with a small number of cities so each chapter gets the attention it needs. If you’d like to see one where you are, tell us.
